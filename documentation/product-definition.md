Product name: AUTO lights 
Automatic headlight that can be upgraded into a car that wasn't equipped with them. 

Purpose: It allows customers to not worry about properly turning on/off their headlights. While this feature is in many vehicles, old ones lack access to it. So for those who like the style of the old cars but want the features of the newer models this is for them. 

 
Objective:  Giving one of the commodities of newer cars to their older models. It allows users to have one less thing to do while driving. As long as they turn the switch into the Auto position they won't have to worry about turning their headlights on or off. 

User interaction Stories
	Unboxing:  Just like aftermarket radios it is recommended to go to a shop to get it installed. A customer may install it but that will void the warranty. 
Configuration: The 3 position rotary switch would be attached to the car dash at an accessible spot. One photolytic resistor would also be added to the top of the dash to be able to detect the darkness level. While the micro controller would be inside the dash. The outputs going to the headlights 
Using:  The only thing the customer has to do is setting the switch to their desired position. As the rest is passive and is dependent on the environment the car is at. 

User Interface: The switch is labeled with Auto, ON and OFF. In that corresponding order. 

User Acceptance:  Lights turn on at a corresponding darkness level about 30 min before sundown and turn off 30 minutes after sunrise. Which is the legal requirement in NJ for having your lights on. 

Technical:  Being inside a car it needs to be able to handle harsh temperatures.
Must be protected against humidity 

Operations: The Auto lights system may only start working after the ACC position is reached with the ignition switch of the car as to not drain the battery. 

Regulatory:  This product will have the lights on while dark but in situations like fog or rain. Customers may have to manually turn the switch into the on position by themselves. As it may not be dark enough. 

Environment:  Being inside of a car the equipment must be able to reliably work while under high and low temperatures. As well as not being susceptible to dust and moisture. Power for this will be provided by the battery. 

Key Concerns: Keeping everything sealed so that debris and moisture won't be able to interact with components. In case of failure override of the microcontroller is made by manual selection. Power drainage from the battery is at most 5% greater than the regular headlights. 

Future: hazardous condition for automatic fog lights or headlights in situations where it isn't dark but they may be needed.
