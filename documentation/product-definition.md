Product name: Friendly Headlamp 
A smart headlamp that can dim itself. 

Purpose: It allows customers not to worry about blinding other people while wearing the headlamp since it's able to auto-dim. 

 
Objective:  Create a safer headlamp and reduce the amount of eye damage caused by the sudden flashing of a bright LED. Having this headlamp reduces one worry of where to look while working in groups, and allows you to have it on and not turn it on and off.


Future: adding a distance sensor so that it only triggers at a  certain distance.
